Booking Workflow with Conditional Follow-Up

Fires when a customer books an appointment. Confirmation email goes out immediately, then a reminder the day before. From there a condition splits the path: if the reminder email was opened, the flow ends quietly. Automation

Appointment Confirmation & Reminder Sequence

A full reminder ladder off a single booked-appointment trigger: confirmation email and SMS straight away, a 24 hour reminder SMS, then a final SMS one hour out. After the appointment it waits, then sends a feedback request email, which is what feeds the review pipeline later.

app.gohighlevel.com / automation / no-show-recovery
Automation

No-Show Recovery Workflow

Triggered when an appointment is marked no-show. A rebooking email goes out first, then the flow waits a day and splits: if the email was engaged with, it closes out. If it times out with no click, an SMS reminder goes out and a task is created for the team, so a missed appointment gets a second chance instead of being written off.
